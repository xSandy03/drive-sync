Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Drive Sync is a personal tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Google Drive to copy the owner's own files between Google Drive and computers the owner controls.

  • No data is collected from anyone else.
  • Files and Google account data are not sold, shared or sent to any third party.
  • Google Drive data is used only to keep the owner's files in sync, and is stored only in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's own computers.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
